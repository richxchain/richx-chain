<h1 align="center">Bitcoin RXC (RBTX)</h1>

Bitcoin RXC is a proof-of-work token on RichX Chain that follows the issuance design Satoshi Nakamoto described: a hard cap of 21,000,000 and a halving schedule. The contract has no owner and no admin functions. **Public mining is not open yet. It is planned for 2027.** Until then no mining software or mining instructions are published here.
